About the records

The IAU Working Group for Planetary System Nomenclature approves feature names. USGS publishes the Gazetteer. This edition includes approved Moon and Mars records with mapped centres in its nightly GIS exports. Country and cultural group labels describe the origin of a name. The lunar satellite category contains lettered features named after nearby prominent craters.
